WARNING: OSM data is NOT suitable for tracking absolute business counts over time.

The osm_business_timeseries.json file was generated from the OpenStreetMap
historical API (ohsome.org) and reflects the GROWTH OF OSM MAPPING COVERAGE,
not real-world business counts. In 2010, Venice's OSM data was sparse (few 
mappers had contributed), so counts were very low. Over time as more volunteers
added businesses to OSM, the numbers grew — but this reflects mapping activity,
not actual business presence.

This data has been retained as a methodological note but MUST NOT be used to
make claims about real business counts in any city.
